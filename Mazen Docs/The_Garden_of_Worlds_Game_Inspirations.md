# The Garden of Worlds: Game Inspirations

> This living document catalogs creative influences for **The Garden of
> Worlds**. The intent is not to imitate these works, but to identify
> the design principles that make them compelling and reinterpret those
> ideas into a unique universe.

# Part I - Video Game Inspirations

## The Witness

-   Knowledge is progression.
-   Environmental teaching.
-   Landscapes become puzzles.

## The Talos Principle

-   Philosophy woven into gameplay.
-   Ancient ruins beside advanced technology.
-   Questions matter more than answers.

## Outer Wilds

-   Curiosity-driven exploration.
-   Non-linear narrative.
-   Every discovery explains another.

## Myst & Riven

-   Believable civilizations.
-   Environmental storytelling.
-   Machinery built for real purposes.

## Fez

-   Hidden dimensions.
-   Secret writing systems.
-   Ancient mysteries hidden in plain sight.

## Portal

-   Elegant mechanic introduction.
-   Layered problem solving.
-   Humor balanced with scientific experimentation.

## Blue Prince

-   Architecture as gameplay.
-   Discovery through repeated exploration.
-   Spatial reasoning.

## Minecraft

-   Player creativity.
-   Emergent systems.
-   Building research stations instead of simply bases.

## Additional Games

### Heaven's Vault

Ancient language translation as gameplay.

### Chants of Sennaar

Communication itself becomes a puzzle.

### Return of the Obra Dinn

Deduction from evidence rather than exposition.

### Tunic

Knowledge hidden in manuals and symbols.

### Subnautica

Wonder mixed with danger.

### No Man's Sky

Planetary diversity while favoring handcrafted worlds over procedural
excess.

# Part II - Film and Television Inspirations

## Stargate

Perhaps the closest inspiration for travel.

### Ideas

-   Ancient portal network.
-   Multiple worlds connected without conventional spaceflight.
-   Ancient precursor civilization.
-   Archaeology leading to advanced technology.
-   Individual planets each developing distinct cultures.

Garden twist: The portals are not transportation devices alone. They are
an ancient information network whose routing language becomes a major
gameplay system.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Star Trek

Especially:

-   The Next Generation
-   The Original Series
-   Deep Space Nine

### Inspiration

Episodes such as **"The Chase"** demonstrate galaxy-spanning mysteries
left by precursor civilizations.

Ideas to borrow:

-   Scientific problem solving.
-   First contact.
-   Ancient civilizations.
-   Ethical dilemmas.
-   Exploration over conquest.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part IV - Shared Themes

Across nearly all of these works appear recurring ideas:

-   Ancient precursor civilizations
-   Archaeology as adventure
-   Knowledge as power
-   Languages and translation
-   Exploration over combat
-   Scientific curiosity
-   Environmental storytelling
-   Monumental architecture
-   Beautiful mysteries
-   Worlds with coherent ecological and historical identities

# Guiding Principle

The Garden of Worlds should leave players with the feeling that they
have completed the greatest archaeological expedition in history.

The reward is not simply discovering who built the worlds.

It is learning **how they thought**, **why they built them**, and
ultimately deciding what humanity should do with that inheritance.
