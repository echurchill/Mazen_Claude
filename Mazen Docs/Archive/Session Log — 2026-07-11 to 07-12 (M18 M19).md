## M18 — Densified-grid movement & solidity (DONE, Eddie-verified)
**The bet:** keep the tile anchor, free the offset. Instead of continuous physics, **densify the
stand points** (3×3 → a d×d grid) and make solidity = *removing stand points under walls/props*.
The freeform plan was kept as the aspirational north star.

- **Phase 0 — densify (behaviour-identical).** A per-world `standGrid` (odd multiple of 3) with
  pace normalized to density; props keep the 3×3 author grid, only *standing* got finer.
- **Phase 1 — open the grass.** Walkable-by-default; walls claim their border cells; crossings
  from any gap cell with lateral position preserved (incl. diagonals), proven with
  world-position-pinned seam/fold tests. Added the `.natural` wall-less test world (**B** key).
- **Phase 2 — solid props.** `Prop.blocks()` removes the k×k stand block of a prop's author cell;
  a **connectivity guard** (BFS over every authored tile) proves no footprint can sever a tile —
  opening the grass is what saves it (you detour through interior grass).
- **Phase 3 — density = 15** (Eddie: "That feels nice", ~1.3 m/step). Open-field feel pass
  passed the M19 gate: 8-way heading reads as *ground*, not a grid.
- **Known limit:** cube-corner traversal glitch (three tangent frames disagree at a triple-point).
  Accepted — steer players away with props/story (see [Known Issues](../Known%20Issues.md)). This
  later became the driver for the corner-density thickets.

## M19 — the worlds (built pass by pass, all Eddie-eyeballed)

### Natureworld (Earth)
- **First pass:** `TerrainKind` (grass/water/regolith); grass = full-tile `fieldFloor` + shader
  material 14; an unwalkable meandering **water** stream (the natural world's routing in place of
  hedges); **conifers** (cone-on-trunk, crown walk-through, trunk solid); authored roundness 1.0
  → a green planet. Eddie: **"Looks good."**
- **Richness pass:** denser forest, per-conifer green variety, a **lake**.
- **Earth nature pass:** grass **colour zones** (meadow ↔ forest floor, world-space fbm) + fine
  grain; ground **undergrowth** (bushes = topiary, field rocks = buried boulders).
- **Groves:** each tree scatters as **several conifers at random positions per tile** (a distinct
  arrangement every time), clumped by a smooth low-frequency **patch field** (dense stands with
  open meadow between).

### The Moon
- Was rendering as a **green hedge cube** in Earth's sky (the placeholder demo world). Rebuilt as
  `.lunar`: open grey **regolith** (material 16), scattered **boulders**, roundness 1.0 — fixing
  the killer visual (grey moon, not a green cube), same world you visit with **O**.
- **Boulders settle into the regolith** (Eddie, from Apollo photos): centred at ground level with
  the buried half clamped flat, so only the top emerges — never pokes below ground at any angle.
- **Gravel + varied greys:** procedural regolith (dusty undulation + grain + pebble speckle,
  later + micro-grain and fine grit); per-rock grey; per-instance **size jitter** so a rock field
  reads as many distinct sizes.

### Relief / hills (the big one — done collaboratively with Eddie watching)
- **CPU half first (defaulted OFF):** a smooth radial height field `reliefHeight(dir)` +
  `reliefAmplitude`, pinned by headless tests, a strict no-op at amplitude 0.
- **GPU half:** `reliefAmplitude` added to the shared `InstanceData` C struct (no Swift/Metal
  skew), `m14bReliefHeight` transcribed byte-identical, threaded through `m14bInflate` /
  `m14bTransform` / both vertex shaders. CPU-placed things (camera, marker, rigid assets) get
  relief via `inflatedPlacement`, so **the camera sits on the ground by construction**. Live
  tuning on the active world: `,` / `.`. Earth 0.05, Moon 0.08. Eddie: **"I liked the hills."**
- **Craters:** localized bowl dents + subtle rims baked into the shared height field at fixed
  direction constants — read as craters on the grey moon and gentle **hollows/dells** on green
  earth, riding the relief plumbing with no new uniforms.

### Corner-density thickets (Eddie's idea)
Cover thickens toward the eight cube corners on both worlds (a `cornerBoost` from tile direction),
so the glitchy triple-points get walled off by dense trees / rubble — but driven by the random
hash so the edge is soft, not an obvious ring. Later bumped heavier (boost weight 0.55 → 0.90).

## Three bugs Eddie caught from orbit-view screenshots (all fixed)
1. **Ground texture drifted while the world spun** — the procedural materials sampled post-spin
   `worldPosition`. Fix: output the **pre-spin (surface-fixed) position** and sample noise there,
   gluing the texture to the ground.
2. **Black strips on the natural planet** — the dark cubie-**frame rails** were drawn on every
   tile (relief made them worse). Fix: skip the frame on non-maze terrain — a natural planet has
   no cube frame.
3. **Twisting a slice teleported home** — `finalizeSliceRotation` calls the same "arrived" routine
   that fires the walk-through portal, and the moon's return portal sits by spawn, so a twist
   rotated it under the player. Fix: gate the portal trigger to actual movement (`viaMove`).

## Durable lessons reinforced
- **One shared C struct** (`ShaderTypes.h`) for Swift+Metal removes layout-skew risk — what made
  the relief GPU wiring safe.
- **CPU/GPU parallel math** (inflation, relief, crater field) must be transcribed byte-identical
  and pinned by CPU golden tests; the runtime match is the one thing only eyes can confirm.
- **Seam continuity is free** when a field is a pure function of surface direction.
- Never claim a visual is right without seeing it; boot-into-world smoke tests catch crashes, not
  correctness. Always revert debug boots before committing.
