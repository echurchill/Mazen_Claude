# The Garden of Worlds (Working Title)

## High Concept

Long before recorded history, an unimaginably ancient civilization
constructed hundreds of star systems, engineered ecosystems, and guided
the development of intelligent life.

Then they vanished.

Your world has just discovered the first Portal.

Stepping through reveals that your home is not merely one planet among
many, but one room inside a colossal laboratory spanning dozens of solar
systems.

Every world contains fragments of an answer.

No world contains the whole truth.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Design Philosophy

This is a **Knowledge Game**.

The player's most valuable resource is not experience points.

It is understanding.

The player never becomes dramatically stronger.

They become dramatically smarter.

Knowledge permanently changes what the player sees.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# World Architecture

The game universe contains approximately twenty handcrafted solar
systems.

-   Around **20 solar systems**
-   Each contains **3 to 8 planets**
-   Some have **binary suns**
-   Roughly **100 to 120 explorable worlds**

Each solar system feels like a chapter.

Each planet feels like a puzzle.

Each moon feels like a footnote.

## Solar System Themes

-   Biological Garden
-   Machine Nursery
-   Archive System
-   Failed Experiment
-   Observatory
-   Forge

Each system explores a different historical or philosophical idea rather
than simply a different biome.

## Planet Types

### Natural Worlds

-   Forests
-   Oceans
-   Ice planets
-   Deserts
-   Volcanic worlds
-   Floating islands
-   Crystal ecosystems

### Engineered Worlds

-   Planet-wide factories
-   Artificial oceans
-   Weather control systems
-   Orbital elevators
-   Gravity manipulation facilities
-   Planet-sized laboratories

### Hybrid Worlds

Nature and machinery have become inseparable.

Examples include jungles over ancient cities, robots tending forests,
and wildlife using forgotten transit systems.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Portal Network

Instead of faster-than-light travel, civilizations move through an
ancient Portal Network inspired by the feel of Stargates and the TARDIS.

Initially, portals reach only a few nearby worlds.

As players decipher Builder mathematics and routing languages they
unlock additional destinations, eventually learning to construct custom
routes and discover hidden star systems.

Knowledge literally becomes transportation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Exploration Loop

1.  Visit a world.
2.  Observe carefully.
3.  Gather clues.
4.  Translate discoveries.
5.  Form theories.
6.  Unlock new destinations.
7.  Return to earlier worlds with new understanding.

Information functions like equipment.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Ancient Technology

Ancient devices have no instruction manuals.

Every machine must be understood through experimentation.

Clues discovered on multiple planets combine into functional knowledge.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Civilization Layers

Every world contains multiple eras.

-   Modern inhabitants
-   Ancient civilizations
-   Builder infrastructure
-   Evidence of an even older precursor

The deeper the player investigates, the older history becomes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Environmental Storytelling

History is reconstructed through:

-   Architecture
-   Astronomy
-   Genetics
-   Machine design
-   Languages
-   Fossils
-   Geological evidence
-   Orbital engineering

Different scientific disciplines reinforce one another.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Living and Mechanical Ecosystems

Natural ecosystems evolve.

Machine ecosystems evolve too.

Maintenance drones repair factories.

Factories manufacture repair drones.

Artificial pollinators maintain engineered forests.

The line between biology and machinery slowly disappears.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Knowledge Progression

Observation

↓

Pattern Recognition

↓

Theory

↓

Verification

↓

Mastery

Every major discovery permanently expands what the player can perceive
and interact with.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# The Central Mystery

The player's questions evolve:

-   What happened to the Builders?
-   Why did they disappear?
-   Did they actually disappear?
-   What defines a civilization?

The answers continually reshape the player's understanding of the
galaxy.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Endgame

The twenty solar systems were intentionally isolated.

Each represented a different experiment in the evolution of
intelligence.

The player eventually realizes that reconnecting them completes an
unfinished experiment millions of years old.

The final revelation is that the greatest artifact was never the portal
network itself.

It was the conversation between civilizations that the network was
designed to create.

Future expansions can introduce entirely new solar systems, each
representing another experiment and another perspective on life, memory,
intelligence, and civilization.
